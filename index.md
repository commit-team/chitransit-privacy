# Loopgoer Privacy Policy

**Effective October 21, 2026**

Loopgoer is operated by **Commit Ventures LLC**, an Illinois limited liability company
("Commit Ventures", "we", "us"). It shows live Chicago Transit Authority train positions,
arrival times, service alerts, and journey plans. This policy explains what the app does with information. It is
short because the app does very little.

**Privacy Policy** &middot; [Terms of Service](https://commit-team.github.io/chitransit-privacy/terms/)

## The short version

**Loopgoer has no accounts and collects no personal information.** There is nothing to sign
in to, nothing to sign up for, and no profile. Your location is used to show what is near you and to plan trips from
where you are; we keep no history of where you have been. Saved stations and places stay on
your phone.

## We do not collect

- Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account identifiers — the app has no accounts.
- Advertising identifiers. Loopgoer contains no advertising networks or ad-tracking software,
  and no analytics, attribution, or crash-reporting software. Sponsored places in Explore are
  described under [Sponsored places](#sponsored-places).
- Contacts, photos, camera, microphone, calendar, or files.
- Biometric identifiers or information of any kind. Nothing in Loopgoer collects, stores,
  or uses biometrics, so the Illinois Biometric Information Privacy Act does not apply to it.
- Any history, on any server of ours, of the stations you view, the trips you plan, or where
  you have been.

## Location

Loopgoer asks for **approximate and precise location while you are using the app**. It never
asks for background location and cannot access your location when the app is closed.

Location is used to:

1. Centre the map on you when you press the locate button.
2. Show stations, stops and places near you — in Arrivals, in Explore, and on the map, including
   the map's star when a sponsored business is a short walk away.
3. Plan a trip that starts where you are, and suggest addresses near you as you type.
4. Fill in the address when you save where you are as a place, such as Home.

Some of this happens **entirely on your device**: centring the map, and choosing the nearby
'L' stations, places and sponsored businesses to show. The rest needs our servers — finding
nearby bus stops, planning a trip from where you are, timing its walks and bike rides, and
looking up addresses. For those, the app sends the coordinates of that one point with the
request, and no name, account or device identifier. Address lookups are passed on to LocationIQ, our
address-search provider.

**We do not keep a history of your location.** A location sent with a request is used to answer
that request; it is not saved to a database or linked to you, though an answer may be cached for
a short time so the same request is quicker the next time. Declining the permission is fine —
the map, arrivals, alerts, and planning from a station or an address all keep working; only the
conveniences above stop.

## Stored on your device

Saved stations, saved places such as Home and Work, recent trips, and preferences such as light
or dark mode are written to the app's private storage on your phone. This never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

## What our servers receive

To show live transit information, the app asks our service at `chitransit.onrender.com` for the
station, route, journey, or place you are currently looking at — with a location only for the
features described under [Location](#location) — together with a key that identifies the *app*,
not you. As with any internet service, our host processes the network information
inherent in delivering a response, including your IP address, and uses it to serve the request
and to limit abuse, and may keep short-lived technical logs for security and troubleshooting. We
do not use it to build a profile of you.

**We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.** We have
never done so.

## Sponsored places

Some places in Explore are local businesses that pay Commit Ventures LLC to be featured. They are
labelled **Sponsored** and may show an offer or a coupon. Which sponsored places you see —
including the map's star for one within a short walk — is worked out **on your device**, from
place information the app has already downloaded. The app does not record or report who sees
or taps a sponsored place, and nothing about you is passed to the business.

## Others involved

| Service | What it receives | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Render (United States) | Requests to the Loopgoer service, with IP address at the network level | Runs our backend |
| Neon (United States) | CTA station and timetable reference data only — nothing about you | Stores transit data |
| MapTiler | Requests for map tiles covering the area you are viewing, with IP address | Draws the base map |
| LocationIQ (United States) | Address searches, and the coordinates being looked up, sent by our servers — not your IP address or anything that identifies you | Finds addresses |
| Photo hosts, mainly Wikimedia Commons | Requests for the photos of places in Explore, with IP address | Shows place photos |
| Chicago Transit Authority | Nothing about you — our servers ask, your device never does | Source of live transit data |

Your device contacts MapTiler directly for map imagery, and the photo hosts directly for place
photos. MapTiler's policy is at <https://www.maptiler.com/privacy-policy/>, LocationIQ's at
<https://locationiq.com/privacy>, and Wikimedia's at
<https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Privacy_policy>.

## Children

Loopgoer is a general-audience transit app, not directed at children, and collects no
personal information from anyone — children included.

## Your choices

- **Location:** grant or revoke it any time in your device settings.
- **Stored data:** clearing the app's storage or uninstalling removes everything it kept.

Because we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing for us to look up, export,
correct, or delete on request — everything Loopgoer keeps about you is already on your own
phone and under your control.
